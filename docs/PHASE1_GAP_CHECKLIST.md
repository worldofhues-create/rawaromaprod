## Honest verdict — aligned, not derailed (but shallow)
**The platform is ALIGNED, not derailed — but today it largely *shows* the business rather than *runs* it.** The whole 20-stage operational spine is built AND populated with real data end-to-end: procurement (PR→RFQ→quotation→PO→acknowledge), goods-receipt (gate→GRN→auto-batch→QC→release), the formula vault (per-formula ACL + correct alias masking), production (order→pick→issue→mix→oil-batch→QC), packaging (SKU→order→fill→FG→QC), and sales/dispatch — all have working transactional endpoints, real status transitions with audit rows, and live records. The security invariants hold (only the owner reads real formulas; the floor sees masked aliases; traceability is owner-gated; a tamper-evident audit schema exists). **The gaps are about DEPTH and USABILITY, not a broken flow:**

1. **Notifications/alerts are half-wired** — QC *FAIL* emails, but QC *HOLD*, low-stock, expiry, pending-approval reminders, PO-acknowledgement chases, and the 24-hour PO auto-approve escalation do **not**, and everything goes to one hardcoded inbox instead of the responsible role. *(This is exactly the "notify the right person the moment something's wrong" ask.)*
2. **Nothing can be edited, corrected, or deactivated** — there is **no PUT/PATCH/DELETE in any cluster**; every master, document and record is create-once-then-frozen. A typo in a PO/material/bin is permanent.
3. **Document Management with expiry reminders doesn't exist** — mandatory for a regulated factory (vendor licences, material COAs, allergen declarations).
4. **Planning intelligence is absent** — no shortage/reorder calc, no available=on-hand−reserved netting, no FEFO/expiry, no vendor rate-history/scorecard.
5. **Many built endpoints have no operator screen** — Create-Role/Assign-Permissions, quotation entry, vendor contacts/mapping, geo masters are route-only.
6. **Search is client-side over one loaded page** — fine at demo scale, unusable at 10k rows.

In short: the skeleton and muscle are in place and correct; what's missing is the **nervous system (alerts)** and the **hands (edit/correct/manage/print)**. This is a finishing job, not a rebuild.
